# Limited paid-program protocol

Proposed only. Explicit authorization is required before invitations, account enrollment, ads, charges or any spending. This protocol describes deliverable research services, not a future native app. The master [validation plan](../Validation-Plan.md) governs decisions when track-level illustrative thresholds differ.

## What can honestly be sold

**C2 component service — $149 once:** a human-reviewed work capsule using up to 20 relevant owned items and three actual work situations, one explanation/correction round, and a budgeted gap list. No shopping, tailoring fees or garments are included.

**C2 compound service — $199 once for four weeks:** the same capsule plus a practical wear/commute rehearsal, workday and clothing-care adjustments, and two scheduled feedback rounds. Planned total stylist work 90 minutes; ordinary support 30 minutes. This time budget includes intake, delivery and corrections. It is not unlimited personal styling. A participant who needs more service gets a transparent scope discussion; extra research rescue time is logged and costed even when not charged.

**C3 component service — $199 once for eight weeks:** qualified trip-conditioned preparation based on confirmed operator demands, scheduled reviews and ordinary program questions. No medical clearance, trip booking, guiding or equipment purchases are included.

**C3 compound service — $299 once for eight weeks:** the same preparation plus two explicit equipment shakedowns and a practical packing/preparation reconciliation. Planned total expert work 120 minutes and support 30 minutes. An appropriate professional approves activity scope and changes. This is not emergency support or assurance that a traveler can complete a route.

The two alternatives must be genuine, available, competently delivered choices. Do not force equal numbers by calling assigned free participants paying customers. Record the full offered denominator, which service was selected and why. Serve up to ten customers per concept within confirmed capacity; stop accepting orders when capacity is exhausted. If only one concept passes earlier stages, test only that one.

## Preflight before authorized selling

Finalize the service provider's identity and qualifications, actual rate, content rights, dates, cancellation/refund terms, data notice and contact route. Confirm the exact deliverable and capacity before accepting a payment. Publish neither claims of validated outcomes nor app screenshots suggesting available software. State “human-assisted program using existing digital tools; no native app included.”

Use independent web acquisition/payment for the service if chosen and permitted. A later app-origin digital sale has different store requirements; this pilot is not a method of assuming store fees away. Stripe card charges retain original processing fees after refunds and initial payouts can take 7–14 days or longer. Reconcile bank receipts rather than counting charge confirmations. [Stripe fees](https://stripe.com/pricing), [refund terms](https://support.stripe.com/questions/understanding-fees-for-refunded-payments), [payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/payouts).

Proposed consumer-friendly pilot policy for approval: refund before substantive personal work starts; a clearly described remedy or refund if the promised service is not delivered. Do not use a restrictive policy to manufacture low refunds. Final terms should reflect the actual service and applicable requirements. The model's 8% refund provision is an assumption, not permission to refuse a refund above a quota.

## Delivery and observation

**Enrollment:** record study ID, exact service bought, price, offer source, dates, refund terms, separate AI/photo consent and the chosen outcome. Ask the purchase reason before the first session: outcome, convenience, trust in expert, accountability, price, curiosity, or another reason in the customer's own language. Save the answer before interpreting it.

**C2 enrollment timing:** start the four-week paid service about two weeks before a confirmed return (10–18 days). This is a narrower delivery window than the 4–8 week context-interview screen; record the change so recruitment yield is not overstated. **Week 1:** limited relevant wardrobe intake; first usable outfit; expert check. **Week 2:** rehearsal and one concrete adjustment. **Weeks 3–4:** actual return-day use and two scheduled feedback opportunities. If her return date changes, classify delayed opportunity rather than failed usage, and record the cost of extending support.

**C3 weeks 1–2:** confirm itinerary, professionally scoped preparation and first gear trial. **Weeks 3–6:** agreed preparation, second shakedown and scheduled review. **Weeks 7–8:** finalize what she has actually tested and unresolved operator questions. Collect post-trip feedback only after the trip happens. Program completion is not proof of successful travel or clinical improvement.

Use human-reviewed outputs and ordinary private delivery. No new product code is needed to test the service. Log every AI error, missing/unsupported fact, correction, contact and minute—including background research and reassurance. A founder doing unpaid work is still delivering a service with a replacement cost.

## Measurement definitions

| Measure | Operational definition |
|---|---|
| Qualified offer | A distinct eligible person sees the exact service, price and terms. Not a social impression or email sent |
| Purchase | Successful real charge for the disclosed service; refunds separately linked. Interview incentives/friends/discounts separately tagged |
| Activation | Customer provides enough information and receives the first usable reviewed result; timestamp both sides |
| Connection-caused change | A recorded observation in one concern changes an action in another, beyond the component baseline; reviewer records the supporting artifact and customer confirmation |
| Completion | C2: reviewed preparation plus actual use on at least three relevant workdays. C3: agreed eligible practice minimum plus two gear trials and final reconciliation. Report individualized obligations and opportunity windows |
| Repeat useful use | A second distinct real occasion or review produces an action actually used; reopening the same plan is not automatically another outcome |
| Delivery burden | Expert, support, founder rescue and acquisition minutes separately; both median and 75 th percentile, plus total cost and worst case |
| Refund | Amount, request/processing/bank dates and customer reason; retain later refunds against original acquisition cohort |
| Renewal/rebuy | A fresh voluntary payment after the first service for a new eligible need; intention, saved card and ongoing entitlement excluded |
| Cash received | Bank-settled funds less refunds/fees; outstanding processor/store balance remains receivable |

For a ten-person sample, report every failure reason alongside counts. If fewer than three relevant workdays occur during C2's observation window, mark the outcome as not yet observable and follow up when the opportunity occurs; do not score absent opportunity as failed use. Record any added follow-up cost. Small samples do not justify precision in percentages or confidence about long-term retention.

## The bridge to a digital offer

Only after concierge value is visible, propose a reduced-service offer to fresh prospects: C2 $149/four weeks; C3 $199/eight weeks; clearly disclose fixed human review limits and self-directed delivery. These are the eventual digital-pass prices in the commercial model, not established app prices. Do not imply that the reduced-service offer provides the same level of personal attention.

Before that offer, test whether AI-assisted preparation reduces expert review to 30/45 minutes per case, with support 15/30 minutes and acceptable error correction. If not, change the cost model, price or scope. A qualified reviewer should compare a small blinded sample against the earlier full-service output. Price and demand testing cannot excuse poorer quality.

Five fresh full-price digital-program customers completing useful repeated tasks supplies stronger evidence for scalable delivery than a concierge cohort. It still does not demonstrate native-store conversion, native usage or annual renewal. Record which tasks would benefit from a native capability and whether the customer cares. If the service works perfectly through a small packet and messages, consider that business honestly rather than forcing an app.

## Stop rules

Stop the formulation if the cheaper component consistently delivers the whole outcome, customers mainly purchase expert reassurance, service costs consume contribution, repeat tasks are contrived, or qualified customers cannot be reached at the required economics. Pause the health-related activity immediately if delivery exceeds the professional's agreed scope; refer the question appropriately and include the lost delivery capacity in the business assessment.

The final field report must state: offers, purchases, refunds, eligible completion windows, completed outcomes, incremental connection value, expert/support minutes, total acquisition cost, bank receipts, deferred obligations, and all reasons not to proceed. Report paid service and paid digital cohorts separately. No MVP specification is written merely because the research budget has been used.
