## Research principles

- Existing competitors are evidence to investigate, not an automatic rejection of the market.
- Evaluate execution, customer workarounds and distribution alongside feature lists.
- Separate source-supported facts, individual accounts, inference and proposed experiments.
- Do not infer revenue from ratings, a listed price or audience size.
- Do not generalize a single aesthetic preference to all women.
- Keep clinical claims and data handling consistent with the actual product.
- Use Markdown tables, direct source links, dated findings and descriptive filenames.
- Future research belongs in `research/`; update this index and the synthesis when conclusions change.

## Provenance and limits

The four specialist memos were generated by parallel GPT-6 Astra agents with High reasoning and reviewed by the coordinating agent. Agent agreement is not independent empirical validation. Evidence includes primary studies, official product sources, public reviews and discussions, and archived Mobbin screens. No interviews, private financial records, purchases or live clinical testing were performed.

Research is unrelated to any proprietary AppKittie dataset. The earlier AppKittie Lab prototype is a separate project and has not been copied into this repository.
