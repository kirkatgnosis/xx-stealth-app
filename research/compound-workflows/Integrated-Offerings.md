The meaningful question is not whether these companies have several categories. It is whether their customers accomplish a connected outcome better, buy because of that connection and stay or purchase again. The public sources do not isolate that causal or commercial contribution.

## Baselines the tests must include

**For C2:** a competent Indyx or other personal-styling brief; Whering's current weather/wardrobe planning (free with in-app purchases, not all-free); Stylebook's $4.99 US organization/calendar/packing tool; free retail styling plus actual try-ons; a tailor; familiar notes and shopping/returns. General AI with photos and explicit constraints can also organize a capsule. It cannot verify physical fit. [W06](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/whering-your-digital-closet/id1519461680), [W07](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stylebook/id335709058), [W08](https://www.nordstrom.com/browse/services/personal-stylists/appointments).

**For C3:** operator-provided preparation, free training plans, existing trainer, outdoor shop advice and a practice walk. REI–Intrepid's 2026 arrangement includes packing guidance and gear-related benefits, adding another integrated alternative. Phone-free enjoyment on the trip is a valid preference. [T07](https://fitfortrips.com/pricing/), [T11](https://www.backroads.com/faq/how-do-i-train-my-backroads-trip), [T12](https://www.roadscholar.org/blog/preparing-physically-for-your-road-scholar-program/), [T13](https://www.rei.com/newsroom/article/rei-co-op-and-intrepid-travel-introduce-active-travel-trips-for-members-and-customers).

**For C1:** the existing strength program plus its nutrition/community tools, free Noom support in its GLP-1 scope, appropriate clinicians, familiar meals and a reusable weekly plan. Household cooking/childcare and clinical treatment may be the missing resources; software cannot replace those services. [M06](https://www.noom.com/in-the-news/noom-launches-free-version-of-its-powerful-glp-1-companion/), [M07](https://www.haileyhappensfitness.com/hhfmembership), [M10](https://www.joinladder.com/).

These alternatives should be described accurately in field tests. A weak generic workout, a deliberately uninformed stylist or a blank spreadsheet is not the strongest-component control.
